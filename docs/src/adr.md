# Architecture Decision Records

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) capture the significant technical
decisions made during RGraph's development, along with their context,
consequences, and alternatives considered.

## ADR Index

| ADR | Title | Status |
|-----|-------|--------|
| [ADR-001](adr/001-async-storage-traits.md) | Async Storage Engine Traits | Accepted |

## Template

Each ADR follows this structure:

1. **Status** — Proposed / Accepted / Deprecated / Superseded
2. **Context** — What is the issue that we're seeing?
3. **Decision** — What is the change that we're proposing or have agreed to?
4. **Consequences** — What becomes easier or more difficult to do?
5. **References** — Links to code, tasks, and related documents.
